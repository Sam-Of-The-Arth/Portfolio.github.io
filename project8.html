PROJECT_08 Automotive / Aero

Lexus SC300 Widebody Bodykit & Aero Testing

Automotive Design & Aerodynamics — In Progress

Streamline CFD pass on the redesigned body — checking that the widebody and diffuser actually clean up airflow instead of just looking fast.

Overview

A full widebody kit for a Lexus SC300 — custom front bumper, custom hood, widened fenders, rear diffuser, and a ducktail wing — modeled in Blender and validated with a CFD simulation I built myself rather than trusting the shapes to look right and hoping for the best. The goal is a kit that's functional, not just styled: every panel gets checked against the stock car's airflow before it's treated as finished.

Key Features

  • Custom front bumper redesigned around airflow into the radiator and toward the underbody
  • Custom hood profile integrated with the new front-end bodywork
  • Widebody fender flares for a wider stance and larger tire fitment
  • Rear diffuser for underbody airflow management
  • Ducktail wing for added rear-end stability
  • Full 3D model of both the stock and redesigned car for direct before/after comparison
  • Custom-built CFD simulation (not an off-the-shelf package) to test the kit's actual aerodynamic effect

Technologies / Tools Used

Blender Custom CFD Simulation Aerodynamics Automotive Design Surfacing / Body Modeling

Project Details

I modeled both the stock SC300 and the redesigned widebody version in Blender so I'd have a direct baseline to test against, rather than only ever simulating the new design in isolation. The aero testing runs on a CFD pipeline I wrote myself, which streams airflow across both models so I can actually compare how the front bumper, hood, widebody, diffuser, and wing change the car's airflow instead of guessing from how the panels look.

This is still an active project — the bodykit design and the CFD tooling are both being refined as I go, and I'm adding more renders and test passes as they're done.
